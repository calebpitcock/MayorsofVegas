## What changed (v3.6 → v3.7c), all UNTESTED against real data

### v3.6 — early injury exits (`nfl_build.py`, `overrides.json`, `test_early_exit.py`)
- **Bug:** a player hurt early in a game counted as a full game with a tiny share, and as his most recent game it
  got the most weight (half-life 2). His backups' inflated shares in that game also counted in full, because he was
  "present". Barkley (stinger on the first play of Week 2) came out at 39% of PHI carries vs Bigsby 30%, which created
  fake edges: Bigsby TD +600 at +7.7% EV, and Barkley u70.5 rush at +8.4%.
- **Fix (`EZEXIT=1` default, `0` = old behaviour):** a game is flagged when both hold:
  - his snap share is under 50% of his own baseline (median of his other games for that team, baseline ≥30%);
  - there's injury evidence: play-by-play "was injured during the play" (adds `desc` to PBP_COLS), or he's on the
    injury report in the next two weeks (only what the build week could see), or `overrides.json` `EARLY_EXIT` lists
    him.

  Flagged games are dropped from his snap rows, so they behave exactly like missed games:
  - his shares skip that game;
  - teammates get the usual 0.25 absent-starter down-weight for it;
  - role_adjust treats his next game as a return.

  Benchings with no injury evidence (Stevenson, Week 2) still count.
- **Tested:** unit tests plus a synthetic two-game PHI replica through `Builder.team_players` (Barkley 0.24 → 0.47,
  Bigsby 0.38 → 0.21). **Not tested on real data.**
- **Live slate:** Barkley and Bigsby rush shares were hand-set to 0.55 / 0.138 as an estimate of the fix. The fake
  edges disappeared: Bigsby TD −9.7% EV, Barkley under +1.4%.

### v3.7 — formation run term (`formation.py`, `formation_2026.json`, `engine.js`, `matchups.js`, `pregame.sh`)
- **Inputs:**
  - offense personnel: share of plays with 3+ WR, the rest heavy (2+ TE or 2+ RB);
  - defense tendencies: light box (≤6), stacked box (8+) and nickel/dime rates.
- **Box mix for the matchup:**
  - heavy personnel vs a defense that stays in nickel/dime → lighter boxes;
  - heavy personnel vs a defense that answers with base personnel → heavier boxes;
  - shift constant C = 0.5.
- **Run efficiency:** that box mix is converted to yards per carry using league values by box (light 5.2 / 7-man 4.5
  / 8+ 3.8), relative to a league-average matchup. The effect is:
  - damped to 40%, because box counts partly follow the situation and `dp.ypc` already carries some of this;
  - capped at ±6%;
  - based on 2026 rates shrunk toward league by sample size.
- **Wiring:**
  - the engine multiplies run efficiency by `g.form[side].run ^ ctx.fw` (`ctx.fw` defaults to 1; 0 = off; neutral
    sims use 0);
  - `matchups.js` compares against fw:0 and adds a "Formations:" line to each team's text;
  - `pregame.sh` runs `formation.py` before `precompute`.
- **Size:** small by construction. Tonight CHI +0.5%, PHI −0.1%. Even the most extreme defense (CIN, 69% light boxes)
  is only about +6% raw.

### v3.7b — position shifts (TE/RB targets and yards per target)
- **Definitions:**
  - `bv` = (offense 3-WR rate × defense base rate) − league: linebackers covering a spread set;
  - `nh` = (offense heavy rate × defense nickel/dime rate) − league: a DB on the tight end.
- **Multipliers:**
  - TE targets 1 + 0.6·bv − 0.4·nh; TE yards per target 1 + 0.3·bv − 0.2·nh;
  - RB targets 1 + 0.5·bv; RB yards per target 1 + 0.3·bv;
  - all capped at ±8%.
- **Engine:** `fT` multiplies `wTgt` and `wRZTgt`, so red-zone targets and TDs move too. `fY` multiplies yards per
  catch. WRs absorb the difference through shared target weights.
- **Coefficients are guesses from the mechanism, not fits.** Tonight: PHI TE targets +5.3%, RB +2.2%; CHI TE −4.3%,
  RB −1.8%.

### v3.7c — slot receivers (`slot_2026.json`)
- Each player's share of snaps in the slot. `formation.py` writes per-player shifts to
  `g.form[side].players[name] = {tgt, ypt}`:
  - WR: 1 + 0.6·bv·slot for targets, 1 + 0.3·bv·slot for yards per target;
  - TE: the position shift is re-weighted by (0.5 + slot), so slot/move TEs get the linebacker mismatch and inline
    TEs get less.
- The engine uses the per-player value first, then the position value. It also sets `p.slot` on slate players.
- **The file is empty on the live board.** The StatRankings table couldn't be read from claude.ai (the page's long
  menu truncates the fetch).
