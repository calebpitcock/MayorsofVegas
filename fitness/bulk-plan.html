Moving up

  • Stop each set with 1–2 reps left in the tank.
  • Once every set hits the top of the rep range, add 5 lb on the bar or go to the next dumbbell.
  • Log every set as weight × reps.

When the 50s and 165 get too light

  • Push the reps up to 15–20.
  • Take 3–4 seconds to lower each rep.
  • Pause 2 seconds at the bottom (or squeeze 2 seconds at the top of rows).
  • Once 165 × 8 is easy on bench, pause each rep on your chest and add a 5th set.

Keeping it fresh

  • Cook on Sunday. Keep Monday–Wednesday in the fridge (6 containers).
  • Freeze the other 8 (Thursday–Sunday). Move each day's pair to the fridge the night before.
  • For the fruit flies: keep the sweet potatoes in a closed cabinet and use them all this week. Put open sauces in the fridge.

Stomach and creatine

  • Take 3–5 g of creatine with your 9:00 food, never on an empty stomach, and skip the loading phase.
  • Skip protein bars. Their sugar alcohols and chicory root fiber commonly cause cramps.
  • Eat your 3:00 snack about 2 hours before you lift.
