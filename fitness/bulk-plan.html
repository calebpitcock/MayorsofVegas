    Weights like 40s mean one dumbbell in each hand. Start every session with 5 minutes on the treadmill. Grey boxes show what you actually lifted last time.

    How to progress

    Every week

    1. Stay in the rep range with 1–2 reps left in the tank. Don't grind the big lifts to failure.
    2. Try to add a rep or two each week.
    3. Once every set hits the top of the range, go up: +5 lb on the bar, or the next dumbbell.
    4. Log every set as weight × reps, the same way you logged your first session.

    When the 50s and 165 run out

    Rows and shrugs are already there. Bench will probably get there in 6–10 weeks.

    • More reps: push sets to 15–20.
    • Slower: take 3–4 seconds to lower each rep.
    • Pauses: 2 seconds at the bottom, or a squeeze at the top of rows.
    • 1.5 reps on squats and split squats.
    • Bench at 165 × 8: switch to a 2-second pause on the chest, then add a 5th set.

    Cardio: a 10-minute incline walk on Wednesday and some weekend walking is plenty. More than that makes gaining harder unless you eat more.

    A normal workday

    Nothing gets measured. Every portion is a whole package split evenly, a count, or a handful.

      Sunday bulk cook

      About 75 minutes. Two recipes make 10 containers, which covers lunch and dinner Monday to Friday.

      The routine

      1. Oven to 425°F and get the chicken in.
      2. Big pot of water on for rice or pasta.
      3. Brown the beef while both cook.
      4. Deal everything into containers.
      5. 6 go in the fridge (Mon–Wed). 4 go in the freezer (Thu–Fri).
      6. Wednesday night, move Thursday's containers to the fridge.

      Deal it out like cards

      1. Line up 5 containers.
      2. One big spoonful of rice or pasta in each. Go around until the pot is empty.
      3. Same with the meat, then the veggies.
      4. If one looks light, move a spoonful over. That's all the precision you need.

      Each container: about half carbs, a third meat, the rest veggies.

      Rice with no measuring: boil it like pasta

      Fill your biggest pot ¾ with water, bring it to a boil, and dump in the whole 2-lb bag. Boil 12–15 minutes until a grain is soft, then drain in a colander. That's enough for 10 containers. On pasta week, only the chicken needs rice, so use half the pot and keep the rest for dinners at home.

      Recipes

      Overnight oats, 5 at once

      1. Line up 5 jars and fill each one halfway with oats.
      2. Pour Fairlife in until it just covers the oats, plus a splash.
      3. Add one big spoonful of peanut butter and a squeeze of honey to each.
      4. Stir, lid, fridge. Add a banana or frozen berries in the morning.

      Office rules

      Fine to microwave

      Chicken, beef, turkey, rice, pasta, corn, green beans, carrots, peas

      Never at work

      Fish, eggs (hard-boiled too), broccoli, cauliflower, brussels sprouts, curry, popcorn

      Stomach and creatine

      • Creatine: take 3–5 g with breakfast, never on an empty stomach, with no loading phase.
      • Fairlife instead of regular milk. It's lactose-free, and lactose is a common reason protein powder causes stomach aches.
      • Skip most protein bars. Their sugar alcohols and chicory root fiber are common causes of cramps.
      • Eat the PB&J about 2 hours before lifting, not right before.
      • Go light on beans and broccoli.
