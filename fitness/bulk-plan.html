Moving up

  • Stop each set with 1–2 reps left in the tank.
  • Once every set hits the top of the rep range, add 5 lb on the bar or go to the next dumbbell.
  • Log every set as weight × reps.

When the 50s and 165 get too light

  • Push the reps up to 15–20.
  • Take 3–4 seconds to lower each rep.
  • Pause 2 seconds at the bottom (or squeeze 2 seconds at the top of rows).
  • Once 165 × 8 is easy on bench, pause each rep on your chest and add a 5th set.

Stomach and creatine

  • Take 3–5 g of creatine with breakfast, never on an empty stomach. You don't need a loading phase.
  • Drink Fairlife instead of regular milk. It's lactose-free, and lactose is a common reason protein powder upsets stomachs.
  • Skip most protein bars. Their sugar alcohols and chicory root fiber cause cramps.
  • Keep beans and broccoli light, and eat your 3:30 snack about 2 hours before you lift.
