# Lean Bulk Plan: Workouts, Meals, and Groceries

**For:** 23-year-old male, 165 lb, desk job (8:00 AM to about 5:30 PM), training at the work gym
**Goal:** Gain weight, mostly muscle, and get stronger
**Constraints:** Dumbbells up to 50 lb, barbell bench up to 165 lb, treadmill. Tue–Thu are the best training days. Meals have to be cheap, easy, bulk-cooked, with nothing before work, no fruit, no protein drinks, not smelly at the office, and easy on the stomach. No protein powder. Already taking creatine.

> This is general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. If stomach pain keeps
> happening even after the changes below, see a doctor. Things like lactose intolerance
> are easy to check for.

---

## 1. The numbers

| Target | Amount | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | **About 3,000–3,100 a day** to start | Your maintenance is probably around 2,500–2,700. This gives you a surplus of roughly 400–500. |
| Protein | **140–170 g a day** (about 0.9–1 g per lb) | Enough to build muscle without protein powder |
| Rate of gain | **0.5–1 lb a week** | Faster than this is mostly fat |
| Water | About 3 liters a day | You need more because of creatine and training |
| Sleep | 7–9 hours | This is when you actually recover and grow |

You don't have to count any of this. The meals in sections 4–5 are built to land on these numbers when you follow it as written.

**How to adjust:** Weigh yourself every morning after the bathroom and before eating, then look at the **weekly average**, not individual days.
- You'll probably jump 2–4 lb in the first week or two. That's water, carbs, and creatine, so don't panic.
- After that, if the weekly average hasn't gone up in 2 weeks, add a third burrito at night.
- If you're gaining more than 1.5 lb a week after the first month, drop the night burritos to one.

## 2. Workout plan: 3 days (Tue / Wed / Thu)

Log every session in the **Lift Log** tracker (`fitness/lift-log.html`). It tells you the exact weight and reps to beat next time. You can swap exercises in its "Edit plan" tab. The upper-body days are built from the exercises you've already been doing.

| # | Tuesday: Upper A · Bench | Wednesday: Legs | Thursday: Upper B · Shoulders + Arms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Barbell bench press 4×5–8 | Goblet squat 4×8–12 | Standing overhead DB press 3×8–12 |
| 2 | One-arm DB row 3×12–15 | Bulgarian split squat 3×8–12 / leg | Barbell bench, lighter 3×8–12 |
| 3 | Seated DB shoulder press 3×8–12 | DB hip thrust 3×10–15 | One-arm DB row 3×12–15 |
| 4 | Incline DB fly 3×12–15 | Walking lunge 2×10–12 / leg | Lateral raise 3×12–15 |
| 5 | Lateral raise 3×12–15 | Standing calf raise 3×12–20 | Incline hammer curl 3×10–12 |
| 6 | Incline DB curl 3×10–12 | Plank 3×30–60 sec | Close-grip bench 3×8–12 |
| 7 | Overhead DB triceps extension 3×10–12 | | |

There are no Romanian deadlifts or leg curls. The hip thrust, split squats, and lunges cover your hamstrings and glutes.

**Progression (the tracker does this for you):**
- Stay in the rep range and beat last time by a rep.
- Once every set hits the top of the range, go up 5 lb.
- If you miss the bottom of the range, drop 5 lb.
- At 50 lb dumbbells or 165 on the bar, add reps and lower each rep over 3 seconds.

## 3. Creatine and your stomach

- Take **3–5 g a day with food** (your 9:00 bagel is easiest). An empty stomach and large "loading" doses (20 g a day) are the usual causes of cramps and bloating. You don't need to load.
- Mix it in a full glass of water or milk, and use **micronized creatine monohydrate** if yours doesn't dissolve well.
- Take it every day, including rest days. Timing doesn't matter much.

### Other stomach-friendly rules for this plan

- **Protein powder aches are often lactose.** Whey concentrate and regular milk both have it. This plan has no milk or protein drinks, and string cheese and Greek yogurt are naturally low in lactose.
- **Avoid most protein bars.** Many are loaded with sugar alcohols (erythritol, maltitol, sorbitol) and chicory root/inulin fiber, which are notorious for gas and cramps.
- **Several medium meals beat 2–3 huge ones.** The plan below spreads your food over 5–6 times a day.
- **Go easy on beans, broccoli, cauliflower, and brussels sprouts** in big portions. They cause gas and also smell bad when microwaved.
- **Eat your pre-workout snack about 2 hours before lifting**, not right before.

## 5. This week's recipes (7 containers each)

### BBQ chicken & sweet potatoes (lunch)

| Ingredient | Use all week | Per container |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boneless skinless chicken thighs | 3½ lb raw | ½ lb raw |
| Sweet potatoes, large | 7 | 1 |
| BBQ sauce | 1 cup (about ½ bottle) | 2 Tbsp |
| Frozen green beans | 2 steam bags (12 oz) | about ¾ cup |
| Olive oil | 3 Tbsp | — |
| Butter | 1 stick | 1 pat |

About 845 cal and 50 g protein per container.

### Baked ziti with beef (dinner)

| Ingredient | Use all week |
| --- | --- |
| Ground beef 90/10 | 2 lb |
| Ziti or penne | 2 boxes (1 lb each) |
| Marinara | 2 jars (24 oz each) |
| Shredded mozzarella | 1 bag (8 oz) |
| Parmesan | A shake on each |

About 900 cal and 53 g protein per container. Mix it all together and split it into 7.

**Keeping it fresh:** Keep Monday–Wednesday (6 containers) in the fridge and freeze the other 8. Move each day's pair to the fridge the night before. Keep the sweet potatoes in a closed cabinet and put open sauces in the fridge, so there's nothing out for fruit flies.

**Office rules:** chicken, beef, pasta, sweet potatoes, and green beans are fine to microwave. Keep fish, eggs, broccoli, cauliflower, brussels sprouts, and curry away from the office.

## 7. Weekly checklist

- [ ] Sunday: shop and cook this week's 2 recipes (14 containers, freeze 8)
- [ ] Each morning: grab the lunch container, a bagel, 2 string cheese, a yogurt, and nuts
- [ ] Each night from Wednesday on: move the next day's 2 containers from the freezer to the fridge
- [ ] Tue / Wed / Thu: lift after work and log every set
- [ ] Every morning: weigh in, then check the weekly average on Sunday
- [ ] Creatine every day with your first food
- [ ] 7+ hours of sleep

**What to expect after 12 weeks if you're consistent:** about 6–10 lb gained (mostly muscle, since you're early in your lifting), your bench working sets moving from 145 up to 165, and noticeably bigger shoulders, chest, and arms.
