# Lean Bulk Plan: Workouts, Meals, and Groceries

**For:** 23-year-old male, 165 lb, desk job (8:00 AM to about 5:30 PM), training at the work gym
**Goal:** Gain weight, mostly muscle, and get stronger
**Constraints:** Dumbbells up to 50 lb, barbell bench up to 165 lb, treadmill. Tue–Thu are the best training days. Meals have to be cheap, easy, not smelly at the office, and easy on the stomach. No protein powder. Already taking creatine.

> This is general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. If stomach pain keeps
> happening even after the changes below, see a doctor. Things like lactose intolerance
> are easy to check for.

---

## 1. The numbers

| Target | Amount | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | **About 3,000–3,100 a day** to start | Your maintenance is probably around 2,500–2,700. This gives you a surplus of roughly 400–500. |
| Protein | **140–170 g a day** (about 0.9–1 g per lb) | Enough to build muscle without protein powder |
| Rate of gain | **0.5–1 lb a week** | Faster than this is mostly fat |
| Water | About 3 liters a day | You need more because of creatine and training |
| Sleep | 7–9 hours | This is when you actually recover and grow |

**How to adjust:** Weigh yourself every morning after the bathroom and before eating, then look at the **weekly average**, not individual days.
- You'll probably jump 2–4 lb in the first week or two. That's water, carbs, and creatine, so don't panic.
- After that, if the weekly average hasn't gone up in 2 weeks, **add about 250 calories** (the bedtime snack below, or an extra ½ cup of rice at lunch and dinner).
- If you're gaining more than 1.5 lb a week after the first month, drop about 200 calories.

## 2. Workout plan: 3 days (Tue / Wed / Thu)

**When:** Right after work, around 5:30–6:45 PM. You skip the worst of the traffic and drive home on a smaller rush.

**Why this split:** Training three days in a row means doing the same full-body workout each day would leave you sore and under-recovered. Instead, upper body gets two sessions (Tue and Thu, with 48 hours between them) and legs get their own day in the middle.

| Day | Session | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday | **Upper A:** bench focus | about 60 min |
| Wednesday | **Lower + core** | about 55 min |
| Thursday | **Upper B:** back focus | about 60 min |
| Optional (Mon, Sat, or Sun) | Repeat **Lower + core**, or take a 30-minute walk | about 45 min |

**Every session starts with:** 5 minutes of easy treadmill walking or jogging, then 1–2 light warm-up sets of the first exercise.

### Tuesday: Upper A (bench focus)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Barbell bench press | 4 × 5–8 | 2–3 min |
| 2 | One-arm dumbbell row (knee on bench) | 4 × 8–12 each arm | 90 sec |
| 3 | Seated dumbbell shoulder press | 3 × 8–12 | 90 sec |
| 4 | Incline dumbbell press (or push-ups if the bench doesn't incline) | 3 × 10–12 | 90 sec |
| 5 | Dumbbell lateral raise | 3 × 12–15 | 60 sec |
| 6 | Dumbbell curl | 3 × 10–12 | 60 sec |
| 7 | Overhead dumbbell triceps extension | 3 × 10–12 | 60 sec |

### Wednesday: Lower + core

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Goblet squat (then dumbbell front squat once one 50 is too light) | 4 × 8–12 | 2 min |
| 2 | Dumbbell Romanian deadlift (a 50 in each hand) | 4 × 8–12 | 2 min |
| 3 | Bulgarian split squat (back foot on the bench) | 3 × 8–12 each leg | 90 sec |
| 4 | Dumbbell walking lunge or step-up | 2 × 10–12 each leg | 90 sec |
| 5 | Standing calf raise (holding dumbbells, balls of your feet on a plate) | 3 × 12–20 | 60 sec |
| 6 | Farmer carry (heaviest dumbbells) | 3 × 40–60 steps | 60 sec |
| 7 | Plank | 3 × 30–60 sec | 60 sec |
| — | *Optional:* incline treadmill walk | 10 min | — |

### Thursday: Upper B (back focus)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Chest-supported dumbbell row (lie face down on an incline bench) or bent-over dumbbell row | 4 × 8–12 | 2 min |
| 2 | Dumbbell bench press | 4 × 8–12 | 2 min |
| 3 | Dumbbell pullover | 3 × 10–12 | 90 sec |
| 4 | Arnold press or seated dumbbell shoulder press | 3 × 10–12 | 90 sec |
| 5 | Rear-delt fly (bent over) | 3 × 12–15 | 60 sec |
| 6 | Hammer curl | 3 × 10–12 | 60 sec |
| 7 | Close-grip barbell bench press | 3 × 8–12 | 90 sec |
| 8 | Dumbbell shrug | 2 × 12–15 | 60 sec |

### How to progress ("double progression")

1. Pick a weight you can lift for the **bottom** of the rep range with 1–2 reps left in the tank.
2. Each week, try to add a rep or two.
3. Once you hit the **top** of the range on every set, move up: +5 lb on the bench, or the next dumbbell size.
4. Write down every set: weight × reps. A notes app works fine. If you're not tracking, you're guessing.

### When you run out of weight (50 lb dumbbells, 165 lb bench)

Most of this will happen on legs first. You can keep making progress without heavier equipment:
- **Add reps.** Go up to 15–20 reps. Sets taken close to failure still build muscle.
- **Slow down the lowering** to a 3–4 second count.
- **Pause** at the bottom for 2 seconds (squats, split squats, bench).
- **1.5 reps:** go all the way down, halfway up, back down, then all the way up. That counts as one rep. Great for goblet squats and split squats.
- **Single-leg versions**, like single-leg Romanian deadlifts, make 50 lb feel much heavier.

### Cardio

Keep it light. It's good for your heart and your recovery, but lots of it makes gaining harder. A 10-minute incline walk after Wednesday's session, plus walking on the weekends, is enough. If you add more, eat more.

## 3. Creatine and your stomach

- Take **3–5 g a day with a meal** (breakfast is easiest). An empty stomach and large "loading" doses (20 g a day) are the usual causes of cramps and bloating. You don't need to load.
- Mix it in a full glass of water or milk, and use **micronized creatine monohydrate** if yours doesn't dissolve well.
- Take it every day, including rest days. Timing doesn't matter much.

### Other stomach-friendly rules for this plan

- **Protein powder aches are often lactose.** Whey concentrate and regular milk both have it. This plan uses **Fairlife** (ultra-filtered, lactose-free, about 13 g protein per cup) instead of regular milk. Greek yogurt and hard/string cheese are naturally low in lactose and are usually fine.
- **Avoid most protein bars.** Many are loaded with sugar alcohols (erythritol, maltitol, sorbitol) and chicory root/inulin fiber, which are notorious for gas and cramps.
- **Several medium meals beat 2–3 huge ones.** The plan below spreads your food over 5–6 times a day.
- **Go easy on beans, broccoli, cauliflower, and brussels sprouts** in big portions. They cause gas and also smell bad when microwaved.
- **Eat your pre-workout snack about 2 hours before lifting**, not right before.

## 4. Daily meal template (about 3,100 calories, about 160 g protein)

All numbers are approximate. The idea is a template you repeat, not a menu you need to track to the gram.

| Time | Meal | Where | About kcal | About protein |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6:30 AM | **Overnight oats** (made the night before, eaten at home or in the car) + creatine | Home | 670 | 30 g |
| 10:00 AM | **2 string cheese + a handful (1 oz) of mixed nuts** | Desk | 330 | 19 g |
| 12:30 PM | **Meal-prep bowl #1** (see recipes below) | Work microwave | 750 | 45–50 g |
| 3:30 PM | **PB&J sandwich + banana** (pre-workout) | Desk | 550 | 15 g |
| 7:00 PM | **Meal-prep bowl #2** (a different recipe from lunch) | Home | 750 | 45–50 g |
| *Add if you stall* | **Bedtime:** 1½ cups Fairlife chocolate milk, *or* a bowl of cereal with Fairlife | Home | 200–300 | 15–20 g |
| | **Total (without the bedtime snack)** | | **about 3,050** | **about 155–165 g** |

**Overnight oats (one jar, 5 minutes the night before):**
1 cup rolled oats + 1 cup Fairlife 2% + 2 Tbsp peanut butter + 1 Tbsp honey + a pinch of salt. Stir and refrigerate. Add banana slices or frozen berries in the morning. Eat it cold or microwave it for 90 seconds.

**Rest days and weekends:** Same idea, and you can relax a little. Keep the oats and hit your protein. Eating out is fine, just don't skip meals. Weekends are where most people's bulk quietly falls apart.

## 5. Meal prep: office-friendly recipes

**Rules for office food:**
- **Safe to microwave:** chicken, beef or turkey, rice, pasta, potatoes, corn, green beans, carrots, peas
- **Never microwave at work:** fish, eggs (hard-boiled eggs included), broccoli, cauliflower, brussels sprouts, curry, heavy garlic or onion dishes, popcorn

**Schedule:** Cooked food keeps **3–4 days** in the fridge. So:
- **Sunday (about 75 min):** make 2 recipes, 5 portions each (10 containers). Refrigerate the Mon–Wed portions and **freeze the Thu–Fri portions**.
- The night before you need a frozen container, move it to the fridge to thaw.
- If your work has no fridge, use an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack.

**Portion guide per container:** about 6 oz cooked meat + 1½ cups cooked rice or pasta + ½–1 cup veggies + sauce. Add 1 Tbsp butter or olive oil to the rice for an easy 100+ calories if you're struggling to gain.

### Recipe A: Teriyaki chicken & rice (5 bowls)
- 3 lb boneless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into chunks
- Toss with 1 Tbsp oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Bake at **425°F for 20–25 min** on a sheet pan lined with foil (no pan to scrub).
- Toss with about ¾ cup teriyaki sauce.
- Serve over jasmine rice with frozen corn or green beans (microwave the veggies from frozen).
- **Per bowl:** about 700–750 kcal, about 50 g protein

### Recipe B: Beef & pasta with meat sauce (5 bowls)
- Brown 2 lb ground beef (90/10), then drain.
- Stir in a 24 oz jar of marinara and simmer 5 min.
- Cook 1 lb pasta (any shape) and mix it all together. Top with parmesan.
- **Per bowl:** about 720 kcal, about 48 g protein. Reheats better than almost anything.

### Recipe C: Taco rice bowls (5 bowls)
- Brown 2 lb ground beef or turkey with 2 packets of taco seasoning.
- Serve over rice with corn and shredded cheese. **Pack the salsa separately** and add it after heating.
- **Per bowl:** about 750 kcal, about 45 g protein

### Recipe D: No-cook BBQ chicken bowls (5 bowls, 15 min)
- Shred **2 Costco rotisserie chickens** and mix with BBQ sauce.
- Serve over ready-rice pouches or leftover rice with corn.
- **Per bowl:** about 700 kcal, about 50 g protein. This is the laziest option in the plan.

### Recipe E: No-microwave backup: chicken & cheese wraps
- A large flour tortilla + 5–6 oz rotisserie chicken + shredded cheese + ranch or BBQ sauce, rolled and wrapped in foil. Eat it cold, or heat it in the work oven for 10 min.
- Make 2 per meal. About 700 kcal, about 50 g protein for the pair.

**Rotation idea:** Week 1 is A + B, week 2 is C + D, then repeat. Having a different lunch and dinner keeps you from burning out on the food.

## 7. Weekly checklist

- [ ] Sunday: grocery run and meal prep (2 recipes, 10 containers, freeze 4)
- [ ] Every night: make tomorrow's overnight oats and pack the snacks in your bag
- [ ] Tue / Wed / Thu: lift after work and log every set
- [ ] Every morning: weigh in, then check the weekly average on Sunday
- [ ] Creatine every day with breakfast
- [ ] 7+ hours of sleep

**What to expect after 12 weeks if you're consistent:** about 6–10 lb gained (most of it muscle for a beginner-to-intermediate lifter), your bench up 20–40 lb, and noticeably bigger shoulders, chest, and arms.
