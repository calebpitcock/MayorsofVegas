# Lean Bulk Plan: Workouts, Meals, and Groceries

**For:** 23-year-old male, 165 lb, desk job (8:00 AM to about 5:30 PM), training at the work gym
**Goal:** Gain weight, mostly muscle, and get stronger
**Constraints:** Dumbbells up to 50 lb, barbell bench up to 165 lb, treadmill. Tue–Thu are the best training days. Meals have to be cheap, easy, bulk-cooked, built around pasta, sweet potatoes, and Mexican rice, not smelly at the office, and easy on the stomach. No protein powder. Already taking creatine.

> This is general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. If stomach pain keeps
> happening even after the changes below, see a doctor. Things like lactose intolerance
> are easy to check for.

---

## 1. The numbers

| Target | Amount | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | **About 3,000–3,100 a day** to start | Your maintenance is probably around 2,500–2,700. This gives you a surplus of roughly 400–500. |
| Protein | **140–170 g a day** (about 0.9–1 g per lb) | Enough to build muscle without protein powder |
| Rate of gain | **0.5–1 lb a week** | Faster than this is mostly fat |
| Water | About 3 liters a day | You need more because of creatine and training |
| Sleep | 7–9 hours | This is when you actually recover and grow |

You don't have to count any of this. The meals in sections 4–5 are built to land on these numbers when you follow it as written.

**How to adjust:** Weigh yourself every morning after the bathroom and before eating, then look at the **weekly average**, not individual days.
- You'll probably jump 2–4 lb in the first week or two. That's water, carbs, and creatine, so don't panic.
- After that, if the weekly average hasn't gone up in 2 weeks, add a third burrito at night.
- If you're gaining more than 1.5 lb a week after the first month, drop the night burritos to one.

## 2. Workout plan: 3 days (Tue / Wed / Thu)

**When:** Right after work, around 5:30–6:45 PM. You skip the worst of the traffic.

**Split:** Your focus muscles (thighs, shoulders, arms) are trained on **Tuesday and Thursday**, 48 hours apart. **Wednesday** covers chest, back, calves, and core, so no muscle is trained two days in a row. There are no Romanian deadlifts. Hamstrings and glutes come from hip thrusts, leg curls, lunges, and split squats.

Start each session with 5 minutes on the treadmill. Rest 2 minutes on the first 1–2 exercises each day and 60–90 seconds on the rest. Do the curl and triceps exercise back to back, then rest.

| # | Tuesday: Thighs · Shoulders · Arms | Wednesday: Chest · Back · Core | Thursday: Thighs · Shoulders · Arms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Goblet squat 4×8–12 (40–50) | Barbell bench 4×5–8 (145 lb) | Bulgarian split squat 3×8–12/leg (20s–25s) |
| 2 | DB hip thrust 3×12–15 (50) | One-arm DB row 4×12–15 (50) | Lying DB leg curl 3×10–15 (20–30) |
| 3 | Walking lunge 3×10–12/leg (20s–25s) | Incline DB press 3×8–12 (40s) | Heels-elevated goblet squat 3×12–15 (50) |
| 4 | Seated DB shoulder press 4×8–12 (40s) | Chest-supported DB row 3×10–12 (40s–45s) | Standing overhead DB press 3×8–12 (35s) |
| 5 | Lateral raise 4×12–15 (10s) | DB pullover 3×10–12 (40–50) | Lateral raise 3×12–15 (10s) |
| 6 | Incline DB curl 4×10–12 (20s) | Farmer carry 3×40–60 steps (50s) | Rear-delt fly 3×12–15 (15s) |
| 7 | Overhead triceps extension 4×10–12 (30–35) | Standing calf raise 3×12–20 (40s–50s) | Incline hammer curl 4×10–12 (20s–25s) |
| 8 | — | Plank 3×30–60 sec | Close-grip bench 4×8–12 (115 lb) |

"40s" means one 40 lb dumbbell in each hand. The leg weights are a first guess, so adjust them in week 1.

### Muscle coverage (direct sets per week)

| Muscle | Tue | Wed | Thu | Sets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Thighs (quads)** (focus) | Goblet squat, lunge | — | Bulgarian split squat, heels-up squat | 13 |
| **Hamstrings + glutes** (focus) | Hip thrust, lunge | — | Leg curl, Bulgarian split squat | 6 + lunges |
| **Shoulders** (focus) | Shoulder press, lateral raise | Bench and incline (front delts) | Overhead press, lateral raise, rear-delt fly | 17 |
| **Biceps** (focus) | Incline curl | Rows (indirect) | Hammer curl | 8 + rows |
| **Triceps** (focus) | Overhead extension | Presses (indirect) | Close-grip bench | 8 + presses |
| Chest | — | Bench, incline press | Close-grip (indirect) | 7 |
| Back and lats | — | Two rows, pullover | Rear-delt fly | 10 |
| Traps + forearms | — | Farmer carry | Hammer curl | 3 + curls |
| Calves | — | Calf raise | — | 3 |
| Core | — | Plank, farmer carry | — | 6 |

### How to progress

1. Stop each set with 1–2 reps left in the tank.
2. Once every set hits the top of the rep range, add 5 lb on the bar or go to the next dumbbell.
3. Log every set as weight × reps.

**When the 50s and 165 get too light:** push reps to 15–20, take 3–4 seconds to lower, pause at the bottom of squats, switch hip thrusts and leg curls to one leg, and once 165 × 8 is easy on bench, pause each rep on your chest and add a 5th set.

## 3. Creatine and your stomach

- Take **3–5 g a day with food** (your 9:00 snack is easiest). An empty stomach and large "loading" doses (20 g a day) are the usual causes of cramps and bloating. You don't need to load.
- Mix it in a full glass of water or milk, and use **micronized creatine monohydrate** if yours doesn't dissolve well.
- Take it every day, including rest days. Timing doesn't matter much.

### Other stomach-friendly rules for this plan

- **Protein powder aches are often lactose.** Whey concentrate and regular milk both have it. This plan uses **Fairlife** (ultra-filtered, lactose-free, about 13 g protein per cup) instead of regular milk. Greek yogurt and hard/string cheese are naturally low in lactose and are usually fine.
- **Avoid most protein bars.** Many are loaded with sugar alcohols (erythritol, maltitol, sorbitol) and chicory root/inulin fiber, which are notorious for gas and cramps.
- **Several medium meals beat 2–3 huge ones.** The plan below spreads your food over 5–6 times a day.
- **Go easy on beans, broccoli, cauliflower, and brussels sprouts** in big portions. They cause gas and also smell bad when microwaved.
- **Eat your pre-workout snack about 2 hours before lifting**, not right before.

## 4. Daily eating (about 3,150 calories, about 190 g protein)

Nothing before work. The first food is at your desk.

| Time | What | About |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:00 AM | Bagel + 2 string cheese + Fairlife chocolate milk, plus creatine | 650 cal · 43 g |
| 12:00 PM | Container #1 | 750 cal · 55 g |
| 3:00 PM | Greek yogurt cup + a handful of nuts + a banana (pre-workout) | 415 cal · 21 g |
| 7:00 PM | Container #2, at home after the gym | 750 cal · 50 g |
| 9:00 PM | 2 frozen breakfast burritos, at home | 600 cal · 25 g |
| If you stall | Add a third burrito at night | +300 cal |

**Office rules:** chicken, beef, rice, pasta, sweet potatoes, corn, and green beans are fine to microwave. Keep fish, eggs, broccoli, cauliflower, brussels sprouts, and curry away from the office.

## 5. Meal prep: 3-week rotation

Cook 10 containers on Sunday: 5 of each recipe. Keep 6 in the fridge and freeze 4 for Thursday and Friday. After week 3, start over.

| Week | Lunch | Dinner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Chicken burrito bowls:** chicken, Mexican rice, corn, cheese, salsa on the side (≈800 cal · 60 g) | **Beef pasta with meat sauce:** ground beef, penne, marinara, parmesan (≈720 cal · 48 g) |
| 2 | **Taco beef & sweet potato bowls:** taco beef, 1 sweet potato per container, corn, cheese (≈750 cal · 45 g) | **Chicken pasta:** chicken, penne, marinara or vodka sauce, parmesan (≈750 cal · 60 g) |
| 3 | **Beef & Mexican rice bowls:** taco beef, Mexican rice, corn, cheese, salsa on the side (≈800 cal · 45 g) | **BBQ chicken & sweet potatoes:** BBQ chicken, 1 sweet potato per container, corn or green beans (≈700 cal · 60 g) |

## 7. Weekly checklist

- [ ] Sunday: Kroger run and cook that week's 2 recipes (10 containers, freeze 4)
- [ ] Each morning: grab 2 containers, a yogurt, nuts, a bagel, 2 string cheese, and a banana
- [ ] Wednesday night: move the frozen Thursday containers to the fridge
- [ ] Tue / Wed / Thu: lift after work and log every set
- [ ] Every morning: weigh in, then check the weekly average on Sunday
- [ ] Creatine every day with your first food
- [ ] 7+ hours of sleep

**What to expect after 12 weeks if you're consistent:** about 6–10 lb gained (mostly muscle, since you're early in your lifting), your bench working sets moving from 145 up to 165, and noticeably bigger shoulders, chest, and arms.
